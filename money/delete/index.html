Delete your Solu Money account

Solu Money by Solu Studio works without an account. An account exists only if you signed in with Google to use cloud sync. Deleting it erases everything we store about you.

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open Solu Money.
  2. Go to Settings → Cloud & account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The deletion is immediate. You can choose to keep or erase the data on the phone itself.

Without the app

If the app is no longer installed, email solustudio.app@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my account. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and user ID).
  • The encrypted cloud copy of your data.
  • Premium records linked to the account (purchase confirmations and gifts).

What is not affected

  • Data on your phone, unless you choose to erase it or uninstall the app.
  • A running subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the account does not stop Google from charging.
  • Payment records kept by Google Play under its own terms for tax and accounting.
