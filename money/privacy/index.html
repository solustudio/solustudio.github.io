Solu Money — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Solu Money ("the app") is developed by Solu Studio ("we"), Poland. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Short version: your financial data stays on your phone. If you turn on cloud sync, it is encrypted on your phone before upload, and we cannot read it.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you enter lives in a local database on your phone: accounts, balances, operations, categories, debts, budgets, labels and settings. You can also add an optional profile name and photo. None of this leaves the device unless you turn on cloud sync.

2. Cloud sync (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in with Google:

  • What we receive from Google: your email address and a user ID. We use them only to identify your cloud copy. We do not get access to your Google data, contacts or files.
  • What is stored in the cloud: one copy of your database, end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM). The encryption key is created from your encryption password on your phone, and the recovery code works the same way. Neither your password nor your key is ever sent to us. We, our hosting provider and anyone with access to the server see only encrypted data.
  • Hosting: Supabase (supabase.com) stores the encrypted copy and handles sign-in. Region: EU West (Ireland), eu-west-1 — your data never leaves the European Union.
  • If you lose both your encryption password and your recovery code, nobody can recover the cloud copy. The data on your phone is not affected.

3. Crash reports

When the app hits an error it writes a short technical note. You can see everything it recorded in Settings → Data → Report a problem, and clear it there. If sending is enabled in a release, those same technical notes go to our error-tracking service (Sentry, EU servers). They contain no amounts, names, notes or e-mail addresses.

4. Trading 212 (optional)

If you connect Trading 212, your API key is stored only on your phone, in the system's secure storage. The app sends it directly to Trading 212's servers to read your portfolio value. We never receive the key or your portfolio data.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No ads, no advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No analytics or tracking. Crash reports (when they are sent at all) carry technical details only — device model, system version, app version and the error itself — never your financial data.
  • No access to your bank accounts; all amounts are entered by you.

6. Your rights and controls (GDPR)

  • Export: Settings → Data → export all operations to CSV, or a full backup to a file.
  • Delete: Settings → Cloud → Delete account permanently erases your cloud copy and account. Uninstalling the app erases all data on the phone.
  • Sign out at any time; the app keeps working offline.
  • You can also request deletion or ask questions by email: solustudio.app@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

The legal basis for processing is your consent and the performance of the service you request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO).

7. Security

The app can be locked with a PIN or biometrics, can hide its content in the recent-apps screen, and uses end-to-end encryption for the cloud. Server access is limited so that each user can reach only their own record.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and announce significant changes in the app.

10. Contact

Solu Studio — solustudio.app@gmail.com

Політика конфіденційності (українською)

Коротко: фінансові дані зберігаються на твоєму телефоні. Якщо ти вмикаєш синхронізацію з хмарою, дані шифруються на телефоні ще до відправки, і ми не можемо їх прочитати.

  • На телефоні зберігаються рахунки, операції, борги, бюджети, налаштування, а також ім'я й фото профілю, якщо ти їх додав.
  • Хмара (за бажанням): при вході через Google ми отримуємо лише email та ідентифікатор користувача. У хмарі (Supabase) лежить зашифрована копія (AES-256-GCM). Ключ шифрування створюється з твого пароля на телефоні. Якщо втратити і пароль, і код відновлення, хмарну копію не відновить ніхто.
  • Trading 212: API-ключ зберігається лише на телефоні й передається напряму на сервери Trading 212.
  • Звіти про помилки: лише технічні дані (модель телефона, версія системи, текст помилки). Їх видно в Налаштування → Дані → Повідомити про проблему.
  • Чого немає: реклами, продажу даних, аналітики й стеження.
  • Твої права: експорт (Налаштування → Дані), видалення акаунта (Налаштування → Хмара), запит на пошту solustudio.app@gmail.com. Скаргу можна подати до органу захисту даних (у Польщі це UODO).
