Personal finance, kept private

All your accounts, debts and budgets in one place. The app works fully offline; if you turn on cloud sync, everything is encrypted on your phone first — the server, and we, only ever see ciphertext.

Works without an account

No sign-up needed. Sign in only if you want the data on more than one phone.

End-to-end encrypted cloud

AES-256 with a password only you know, plus a recovery code.

Any currency

Budgets and reports

Monthly limits, spending by category, income against spending over time.

Recurring operations
